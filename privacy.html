Privacy Policy

Interagood Partner Program (Partners and Suppliers) · Last updated: 1 November 2026

Welcome to the Interagood Partner Program. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect your information when you register and use the Interagood mobile application and website as a Partner or a Supplier. By using Interagood you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: full name, mobile number (verified by OTP), username and password (stored in hashed form).
  • Business details: business name, category, address, city, state, pincode, profile photo and banner.
  • Location: your GPS location when you choose to pin your shop (Suppliers) or autofill your address (Partners).
  • Verification details: identity and business verification information such as Aadhaar, PAN or GSTIN where required, and, for Partners, experience, team details and toolbox / team photos.
  • Content you upload: samples, product or work photos and videos, captions and price tags.
  • Subscription details: plan, subscription status and payment reference IDs. Card, UPI and bank credentials are entered on Razorpay and are never stored by Interagood.
  • Technical data: device and app information needed to keep the service secure and working.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and verify your account and show your business profile.
  • To display your profile, samples and location (where applicable) to customers and other users so they can contact you.
  • To send OTPs, lead alerts, payment and renewal notifications.
  • To process your monthly subscription and AutoPay mandate (₹109 for Partners, ₹219 for Suppliers).
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, provide support and comply with legal obligations.

3. App permissions

PermissionWhy we need it

4. Visibility and sharing

  • Your business name, profile photo, banner, category, city, contact options and samples are visible to other users of the platform so that they can find and contact you.
  • We share data with service providers who help us run Interagood: payment processing (Razorpay), cloud hosting and storage, and SMS/OTP delivery. They may use it only to provide their service to us.
  • We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users and the platform.
  • We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Data security

Your data is stored on secure servers and transmitted over encrypted connections. Passwords are stored in hashed form, and access to personal and verification data is restricted. Payment data is handled by Razorpay under its PCI-DSS compliant systems. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your login details private.

6. Retention

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account or cancel AutoPay, your business profile and uploaded files are removed from the platform. We may retain limited records (such as payment and transaction records) as required by law or for fraud prevention and dispute resolution.

7. Your rights

  • Update your profile information from the app at any time.
  • Delete your account and uploaded data from the app, or ask us to do it by emailing support@interagood.in.
  • Request access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you.
  • Withdraw permissions from your phone settings.

8. Children

Interagood is intended for business users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page with the updated date at the top.
