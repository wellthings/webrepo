Frequently asked questions

What is Interagood?

Interagood is a B2B platform and mobile app that connects Partners (skilled professionals) and Suppliers (shops and material sellers) with customers who need them. We provide the platform and communication tools; deals are made directly between you and your customer.

How much does it cost?

The Partner plan is ₹109 per month and the Supplier plan is ₹219 per month, both inclusive of applicable taxes. There are no other charges.

How do I pay?

Payment is made only inside the Interagood mobile app, not on this website. In the app, Razorpay securely collects your payment through UPI AutoPay (e-mandate), debit card or other supported methods. The first charge happens when you authorise the monthly plan, and the plan then renews every month.

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes. You can cancel AutoPay at any time from the app or by emailing support@interagood.in at least 3 business days before the next billing date. Once cancelled, your business profile is removed from the platform and you stop receiving leads.

Will I get a refund?

The monthly fee is non-refundable once charged for an active billing cycle. If you were charged twice or were charged but your plan was not activated, contact us and we will resolve it. See the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Do I need to ship anything?

No. Interagood is a digital subscription service. There is no physical shipping. Your plan is activated in the app right after a successful payment. See Service Delivery.

How is my data protected?

Your data is stored on secure servers and is never sold to third parties. Payment details are handled by Razorpay and are not stored by us. Read the Privacy Policy.
