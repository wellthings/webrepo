Service Delivery Policy

Shipping and delivery information · Last updated: 1 November 2026

1. Digital service, no physical shipping

Interagood sells a digital monthly subscription (Partner ₹109 per month, Supplier ₹219 per month). No physical goods are sold or shipped by Interagood, so there are no shipping charges and no delivery address is required.

2. Activation

  • Subscriptions are paid only inside the Interagood mobile app. No payment is collected on this website.
  • Your plan and business profile are activated in the app immediately after your payment or AutoPay mandate is successfully authorised through Razorpay.
  • A confirmation of the payment is sent to you by Razorpay and the app shows your plan status.
  • If payment succeeded but your plan does not show as active within 24 hours, contact us and we will fix it or refund you as per our Refund & Cancellation Policy.

3. Renewal

The plan renews automatically every month until cancelled. Each renewal is delivered the same way, by keeping your profile active in the app.

4. Goods and work between users

Products sold by Suppliers and work done by Partners are agreed directly between the user and their customer. Interagood does not ship, deliver or take responsibility for those goods or services.
