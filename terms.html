4. Monthly payment (no AutoPay)

There is no AutoPay or recurring mandate. Your plan is paid manually every month for the fee of your plan (₹109 for Partners or ₹219 for Suppliers). Each month you receive a payment reminder before your current cycle ends; you complete the payment in the app to keep your plan active. Your profile stays live and online only while your payment is active. Payments are processed securely by PayU Software Private Limited. Interagood does not store your card number, UPI PIN or bank credentials.

5. Failed or missed payments

If your monthly payment is not completed before the due date, your profile and portal access may be suspended or deactivated until the payment is successfully completed. While suspended you will not receive new leads, orders or deals, and you will not be shown as online.

  • You can stop renewing your monthly plan at any time by simply not paying the next month's fee, or by emailing support@interagood.in.
  • When you stop renewing, your business profile is removed from the platform and you stop receiving leads and deals.
  • See the Refund & Cancellation Policy for full details.

7. Refunds

The monthly subscription fee (₹109 for Partners, ₹219 for Suppliers) is non-refundable once deducted for the active billing cycle, including when you stop renewing or delete your account part-way through a cycle. Refunds are made only for duplicate or erroneous debits, or where payment was taken but the plan was not activated. Details are in the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

8. Account deletion

You can delete your account from the app at any time. Deleting your account (or stopping your monthly renewal) permanently removes your business profile, uploaded photos and videos from the platform. Amounts already charged are not refunded. If you wish to use Interagood again you must create a new account and complete verification again.
