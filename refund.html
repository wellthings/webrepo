Refund & Cancellation Policy

Interagood Partner Program (Partners and Suppliers) · Last updated: 1 November 2026

1. Subscription plans

2. How to cancel

  • In the app: open your Profile menu and cancel AutoPay.
  • By email: write to support@interagood.in from your registered details at least 3 business days before the next billing date.
  • Cancelling stops all future charges. We recommend cancelling in advance so the next cycle is not billed.
Please note: when AutoPay is cancelled or the account is deleted, your business profile is removed from Interagood and you will no longer receive leads, orders or deals.

3. Refund policy

The monthly subscription fee (₹109 for Partners, ₹219 for Suppliers) is non-refundable once it has been deducted for the active billing cycle. This applies if you cancel AutoPay, delete your account or stop using the service during that cycle. Your plan remains valid until the end of the paid cycle unless your account is removed on cancellation or deletion as described above.

4. When we do refund

We will refund the amount in full in these cases:

  • Duplicate payment: you were charged more than once for the same billing cycle.
  • Payment taken but plan not activated: the amount was debited but your plan or profile was not activated because of a technical error on our side.
  • Charge after valid cancellation: you cancelled at least 3 business days before the billing date and were still charged.

5. How to request a refund

  1. Email support@interagood.in within 7 days of the charge.
  2. Include your registered mobile number, the date and amount of the charge and the Razorpay payment ID or a screenshot of the transaction.
  3. We will review your request and reply within 24 to 48 hours.

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method (UPI, card or bank account) through Razorpay and usually reach you within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank.

6. Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, no money is taken. Your profile may be suspended until the payment succeeds. If money was debited but the payment shows as failed, it is normally returned automatically by your bank within 5 to 7 business days. Contact us if it is not.

7. Termination by Interagood

If your account is terminated for fraud, misleading listings or breach of our Terms & Conditions, fees already paid are not refunded.
